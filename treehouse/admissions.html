Admissions

Joining Treehouse starts with a visit

We welcome about 40 new children each year, most into Kindergarten, with a handful of places in Grades 1 to 5. Here is how admissions works, what it costs and how to book a visit.

Admissions process

Five friendly steps

No entrance exams and no interviews for five-year-olds. We want to get to know your child, and for your family to get to know us.

  1. 1

    Visit

    Come to an open morning or book a school-day tour to see classes in action.

    October to December
  2. 2

    Apply

    Complete the online form and pay the $75 fee, which is waived for aid applicants.

    By January 15
  3. 3

    Play visit

    Your child spends a relaxed morning with us: small-group play for Kindergarten, a shadow morning in class for Grades 1–5.

    January and February
  4. 4

    Decision

    Letters go out on the same day to every family, with aid awards included.

    March 6
  5. 5

    Enroll

    Return your enrollment contract and deposit, then join us for the new families’ picnic.

    By March 27

Tuition assistance

1 in 4families receive need-based aid

Awards range from 10% to 85% of tuition and are reviewed each year. Apply alongside your admissions application by January 15; your answers are seen only by our aid committee.

Siblings: 10% off tuition for a second child and 15% for a third.

Included in tuition

  • Books, stationery and classroom materials
  • All field trips and the Grade 5 spring trip
  • Instrument loan for recorder and strings
  • Morning snack every day
  • A school tablet in Grades 3–5

FAQ

Questions parents ask us

How old does my child need to be for Kindergarten?

Children should turn five by September 1 of the year they start. If your child’s birthday falls close to the cut-off, we are glad to talk through readiness with you during the play visit.

Does my child need to read before starting Kindergarten?

No. Children arrive at every stage, from not yet knowing letters to reading picture books. Our small phonics groups meet each child where they are.

Can my child join in the middle of the year?

Yes, when a place is open. Mid-year applicants visit for a shadow morning, and we usually give a decision within two weeks.

How does tuition assistance work?

Aid is based on need, not merit. You complete a short confidential form with your application, and awards are sent with your admissions decision. About one family in four receives help.

How do you support children who learn differently?

Our learning support team includes a reading specialist, a math interventionist and a counselor, with visiting speech and occupational therapists. Please share any reports with us; they help us plan, and they are never a barrier to admission.

Is there a uniform?

No uniform. We ask for clothes that can get muddy in the forest classroom, and a Treehouse T-shirt for field trips, which comes free with enrollment.

Do you offer transportation?

Two morning bus routes serve Maple Hollow and Cedar Falls, and one afternoon route leaves at 3:10 pm. Route maps are in the parent portal.
