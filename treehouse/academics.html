    Learning support

    Help that arrives early, and stretch that goes further

    Every child learns at their own pace. Our learning support team works alongside class teachers so that children who need a boost get it quickly, and children ready for more are never left waiting.

    • Reading intervention

      Groups of three with a trained reading specialist, up to four times a week.

    • Math boost

      Short, targeted sessions that close gaps before they grow.

    • Enrichment

      Extension projects and a weekly math circle for children ready for more challenge.

    • Speech and occupational therapy

      Visiting therapists work on site, so children don’t lose half a day to appointments.

    • School counselor

      Friendship groups, one-to-one check-ins and support for families through big changes.

    How support works

    1. NoticeTeachers spot a need in class work or check-ins.
    2. PlanWe meet with you to agree on goals.
    3. SupportSix weeks of focused help, in or out of class.
    4. ReviewWe check progress together and decide next steps.

    Assessment and reporting

    Clear reports, and no surprises

    We report on skills, not letter grades. You will always know what your child can do, what comes next and how you can help at home.

    • Standards-based

      Each skill is marked on a four-step scale, from beginning to exceeding, so progress is easy to see.

    • A portfolio, not just a score

      Children keep a portfolio of their best work and reflect on it with you at conferences.

    • Light-touch testing

      Short reading and math check-ins three times a year help us plan. No test prep, no stress.
